# §32 — Near-black, not black

The page's ground is `color-background` (#0e0f14), the logo's own dark. It is dark enough for a room
with the lights down and soft enough that text on it does not glare. Surfaces get lighter as they
rise (a field, then a button or a card, then a dialog), so depth reads without a line drawn around
it.

True black (`color-video-background`) appears only behind a film, where it is the picture's frame
and should vanish.

**Bites when:** a panel is pure black, or a raised surface is darker than the one it sits on.

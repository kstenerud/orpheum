> its one accent marks what to do and where you are.

# §31 — The art is the colour; the chrome is neutral

Posters, stills and the film itself are what a person came to see, so they are the colour on every
screen. The chrome around them stays in near-neutral greys (`color-background`, `color-surface`,
`color-surface-raised`), and lets them be.

One accent, `color-accent`, marks three things: the primary action, the current place and progress.
It is never decoration. An accent spent on ornament stops pointing anywhere, and the person loses
the one cue that says which way is forward.

**Bites when:** a screen gets a coloured ground, a second accent, or the accent on something that is
neither the way forward, the place you are nor how far along it is.

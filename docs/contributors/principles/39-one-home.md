> **ABOUTME:** Principle §39: A setting, a problem or a fact is changed in one place; everywhere
> else shows it as a line that leads there.

# §39 — Each thing has one home, and other places link to it

A setting that can be changed in two places drifts: each copy grows its own wording, its own limits
and its own bugs, and the person does not know which to trust. So each thing has one home.
Everything about the sound lives in the Sound menu, and the gear keeps no sound setting (D538 §8).
Each kind of problem has one page where it is fixed. Another place that needs to mention it shows a
line that links to the home rather than a second copy of the control.

**Bites when:** the same setting appears in two menus, or a problem can be fixed from two pages that
disagree about it.

> **ABOUTME:** Principle §33: Coral is go, signal yellow is wrong, red cannot be undone, light blue
> is on; and every meaning a colour carries is also said in words or a mark.

# §33 — Each colour has one meaning, and never carries it alone

Coral (`color-accent`) means go here or do this. Signal yellow (`color-warning`) means something is
wrong or could not be done. Red (`color-irreversible*`) means it cannot be undone. Light blue
(`color-status`) means something is on. None of them stands in for another, so a person who has
learnt a colour once reads it the same everywhere.

A colour is never the only cue: a current choice has its ✓, an error its words and its icon, a state
its label. A person who cannot tell the colours apart, a TV panel that renders them badly, and
forced colours all still read the meaning (WCAG 1.4.1).

**Bites when:** a warning's yellow marks a selection, red marks an error that can be fixed, or a
state is told by colour alone.

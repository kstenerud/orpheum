> **ABOUTME:** Principle §35: Focus is a two-colour ring that may lift what it marks but never dims,
> darkens or covers it, and nothing ever hides it.

# §35 — Focus only adds, and is always seen

On a TV, focus is the pointer: the only way a person knows where they are. It is a two-colour ring
(`focus-ring-inner-color` as the outline, `focus-ring-outer-color` as a ring outside it), so it
shows on any ground, a poster's included. It may add a lift or a modest scale. It never darkens,
dims or covers what it marks, since that is what the person is about to choose. In forced colours it
is the outline alone, in `Highlight`.

Nothing covers it either: not a sticky bar, not the player's overlay, and not a fade that hides the
control it is on (D538 §6 took the scrub bar's fade away for that).

**Bites when:** a part restyles or removes `:focus-visible`, focus dims what it marks, or focus
lands on something hidden behind a bar or an overlay.

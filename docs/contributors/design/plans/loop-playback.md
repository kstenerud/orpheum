## What it is

A person chooses one video, or a set of them, to play in a loop: at the end of the last, the first
plays again, for as long as the player is left on, with no "Still watching?" between.

## Open questions, for the owner when it is designed

1. **What a set is:** a series' season or the whole series, a library or a folder, a collection, a
   hand-picked list, or a queue built in the player.
2. **Where it is turned on:** a control in the player, a choice on a title's or set's page, or both;
   whether it lasts past the session.
3. **What a loop records:** an unattended play marks nothing watched ([play-state.md](play-state.md));
   whether a looped play moves resume positions, marks watched, or counts in Continue Watching at all.
4. **Where it plays:** a browser, a cast to a Chromecast or AirPlay (the receiver's 20-minute wait
   at a film's end, the owner, 2026-11-18), a TV's own browser; and in Watch Together, whose film
   ending with nothing next stops the group (D516).
5. **In order or shuffled.**
6. **Transitions:** a gap-free return to the first video, or the player's ordinary start of the next.

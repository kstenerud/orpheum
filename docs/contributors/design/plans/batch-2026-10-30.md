## 3. To build, in four agents, at most three at once (worktrees; the lead merges)

Each agent's brief names its files and mechanism (memory agent-step-count); each item that only a
device can confirm adds a VQ. The lead makes small fixes found on the way itself.

**A. Cosmetic, the web client's pages** (one agent):
1. The notice that a Watch Together session ended is a modal (the shared ModalDialog), not the
   top-of-page note.
2. A title page's misidentification actions as one "Identified incorrectly" entry, which opens a modal
   offering Rescan, Enter manually and Report this; Play is the page's first button.
3. The rarely used items (Identified incorrectly, Rescan, ordering, …) under a vertical "…" menu.
4. A modal opened from a choice box takes its place, never stacking: one click outside any modal
   returns to the page.
5. The watched check over an episode's still, as on a poster's tray.
6. Watch Together's invite pop-up closes on a click outside it.
7. The "asking to reach devices on your network" note only where the browser will ask (not Silk).

**B. Navigation** (one agent, after A's title and series pages merge, as both touch them):
1. Season posters in place of the season choice box (Jellyfin's), coming back preselecting the season
   left: **D379** (revises D367 §9), a row of season posters on the one series page.
2. The banner's Libraries icon as a menu of the person's libraries, as agreed (one library: straight to
   it; "All libraries" last; the library chooser on a library's page goes; on a TV it closes as any
   chooser does).

**C. Watch Together and the core** (one agent):
1. Item 7 whole, D372 §9–§10: a signed-in guest's page has the banner (tray, menu, Your account); the
   guest stays a member while elsewhere, even playing another film only they see, shown away in the
   roster, and catches up to the shared film on coming back; a guest by link alone may leave the page
   and come back by the same link while the session lasts. (Unblocks VQ103.)
2. A Play page navigated away gives up the lead at once: `pagehide` closes the session's sockets,
   `pageshow` reconnects.
3. The core and its discovery find each other again whatever order they restart in.
4. The `live:` socket's reconnect goes through the session connection's shown-again check (one path).

**D. The player and testing aids** (one agent):
1. **A debug link throttle and connection drop** under Show debug tools: a per-device cap on the
   player's download rate, and "drop the connection for N seconds" (every socket and carrier closed,
   then reconnecting as after a network loss). Chrome's DevTools throttling never reached the WebRTC
   carrier (VQ128's run): this makes VQ102 (2), VQ121 and the Wi-Fi-off checks doable from the page.
   **D380**: the cap in the chunk fetch, tab-only, with a badge while on.
2. A page says when DevTools bypass its service worker (the agreed probe and one line).
3. The cast remote on the sender gets the sound controls (dialogue boost, levelling, the sound track),
   sent to the receiver as commands, and shows the receiver's decision, quality and link.
4. The Fire TV's black blip at a start: the player chooses its first rung from `rememberedLinkKbps`
   before loading anything, so no switch lands as playback begins: **D381** (amends D257 §2).

Order: A, C and D start together; B starts when A's page changes are merged.

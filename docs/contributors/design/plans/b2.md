# B2: the library and its people

- **Status:** PROPOSED — phased 2026-11-21 by the agent from [closed-beta.md](closed-beta.md) B2,
  for the owner to confirm. Phases A and B are specified by existing decisions and can be built at
  once; the rest wait on the questions below, which are the owner's.
- **Depends on:** [closed-beta.md](closed-beta.md), [episode-orderings.md](episode-orderings.md),
  [play-state.md](play-state.md), [parental-controls.md](parental-controls.md), [auth.md](auth.md)

What B2 ends with (closed-beta.md, proposed): genres from TMDB filter the films page; a film marked
watched shows it in its tray and moves out of Continue Watching; a series in DVD order plays in that
order; a missing episode is listed as missing; an ISO plays; switching profile asks for its PIN; a
core account with a second factor cannot sign in by its password alone.

Every phase: behaviour changes carry tests that fail when reverted; `make check-pr` passes at its
end. A schema change is a new migration step: migrations are live from step 86 (D460, rule 8).

## Already built, before B2 started

closed-beta.md's B2 list was written on 2026-10-18; much of it has been built since:

- **Genres and people are kept** (schema.md "Genres and people", step 0067): the identification
  pass writes `genre`, `item_genre`, `person` and `credit` from the stored TMDB response or the NFO.
  A title's and a series' `genres` are in their API bodies (`internal/core/api/item.go`), and
  `GET /genres` lists them with counts (`internal/core/api/people.go`).
- **Genre as a filter** (D417, D470, D480 §1): Films, Series and a library's list have one pop-up
  holding the text filter, Genre, Decade, Unwatched and the sort
  (`web/apps/web/src/list-filters.ts`, `list-chips.tsx`); `GET /items` takes `genre`, `person`,
  `decade` and `unwatched`. **So the first acceptance clause holds today.**
- **More sorts** (D366): name, added, released (films and series), recorded and file name (other
  videos), each either way. The feature-standing audit (feature-standing-2026-10-18.md) counted
  "two sorts, no filters"; that is no longer so.
- **Watched marks and marking watched** (D370): `work_watched`; the white check, the tray's last cue
  (`web/apps/web/src/status-cues.tsx`, `watched-mark.tsx`); *Mark as watched* with Undo on a
  title's, a season's and a series' page (`PUT`/`DELETE /items/{id}/watched` and the collection's
  and season's, `internal/core/api/watched.go`); a film marked watched leaves Continue Watching
  (`TestWatched_AMarkByHandIsAFreshStartAndUndoPutsItBack`). VQ89 passed. **The second clause holds
  today.** D370 §4 put marking on the pages, not the poster ("the tray's icons are never controls"),
  after closed-beta.md's note asked for a toggle in the tray: question 3.
- **Episode orderings, the viewing order** (episode-orderings.md §6 steps 1–4, D159, D210 §3, §5):
  `library.ViewingOrdering` resolves a person's ordering; TMDB's episode groups are listed and
  fetched when chosen; the series page's selector (`GET /collections/{id}`,
  `PUT /collections/{id}/preferences`); Next Up, Up next and the player's previous and next follow
  it (`TestUpNext_IsTheNextEpisodeInThePersonsOrdering`,
  `TestUpNext_PreviousIsTheEpisodeBeforeInThePersonsOrdering`). **A person who chooses a DVD order
  plays the series in it today**; files *numbered* in a DVD order are phase B.
- **Disc images** (D350, D404): `internal/node/disc` reads a DVD's or Blu-ray's `.iso` for its main
  title and the node serves it to FFmpeg through fdproxy. **The ISO clause waits only on
  VQ73–VQ75**, which need the owner's own decrypted images. Disc folders stay skipped
  (`SkipDiscFolder`, `internal/core/library/compare.go`): phase F.
- **A profile is a `user` row** (D432 §4), so every per-person table keyed on `user_id` needs no
  re-keying. Nothing else of profiles exists: no picker, no PIN.
- **The sign-in keeps the second factor's seam** (FEATURES.md F47): `auth.Service.SignIn` checks a
  name and password; nothing checks a second factor.

**One correction:** closed-beta.md says the watchlist's "tables exist". They do not: the baseline
(`0086_baseline.sql`) has no watchlist, favourite or collection-member table, and `sequence` holds
series alone (D426 §2). Phase C adds one.

## Phase A — genres on a title's page (specified — buildable now)

The API already sends a film's and a series' genres; no page shows them.

1. **The line:** a film's page and a series' top page show its genres, in its source's order, each a
   link to Films (or Series) filtered by that genre, the list pop-up's own `genre` address
   (`router.ts`).
2. **Assumptions the build makes** (each the conventional choice; the owner's to change, question
   1): the line sits under the title with its year and runtime; an episode's page shows none (its
   genres are its series'); cast, crew and ratings stay later, as closed-beta.md says.
3. **Files:** `web/apps/web/src/title.tsx`, `title-head.tsx`, `series.tsx`, their tests. No server
   change.
4. **Tests:** a film with two genres shows both in order, and selecting one opens Films filtered by
   it; a title with none shows no line.

## Phase B — episode orderings: refresh, and the files' order (specified — buildable now)

episode-orderings.md §6 steps 5 and 6, as the owner answered its §7 on 2026-10-25.

1. **Refresh (step 5):** a series' refresh refetches its fetched groups and re-materialises them; a
   group no longer listed is kept and marked so. A changed group moves positions and no play state
   (D157); a removed one keeps them.
2. **The files' order (step 6, D210 §1, §7 answers 3 and 6):** an admin sets a series' files' order;
   identification looks each file's numbers up in that ordering's listing and places it in aired
   from the entry (§3.3); the **F** queries of §5 read it. It defaults to aired, and a person who
   never chose a viewing order follows it (D210 §5). Cues — each file's runtime against each
   ordering's episodes, episode titles in file names, two-part files — are offered to the admin as
   a warning when they disagree with aired; nothing is switched on a guess.
3. **Assumption:** the admin's choice is a control on the series' top page, shown to admins alone,
   beside the viewing order's selector.
4. **Files:** `internal/core/identify/refresh.go`, `orderings.go`, `episodes.go` (`positionEpisode`,
   `airedOrderingOf`), `runtimes.go`; `internal/store/queries/orderings.sql` and a migration step (a
   series' files' order, an ordering's "no longer listed"); `internal/core/api/browse.go`;
   `web/apps/web/src/series.tsx`.
5. **Tests:** a library numbered in a fixture's DVD order is named correctly and its aired positions
   are the entries'; it plays in that order for a person who never chose; a removed group keeps its
   positions. This is the DVD clause's acceptance.

Step 7, a person's own ordering, is question 2.

## Phase D — missing episodes (needs the owner's answer to question 5)

D187 §3: "'Missing' is never an action"; nothing offers to get one. episode-orderings.md §3.2: an
episode a listing has and the library lacks has no row, since a position needs a title. The stored
season listings (`provider_response` parts `group/N`) and fetched groups already hold them, but only
for the seasons the library has an episode of (`seasonsToFetch`, `identify/series.go`).

- **Builds:** a season page lists the listing's episodes the library lacks, in their place in the
  person's ordering, marked missing and not playable; they never count toward a season's watched
  check or Next Up, which already read titles alone. Size S for seasons held, S–M with whole missing
  seasons (one request a season, D209).
- **Files:** `internal/core/library/detail.go`, `internal/store/queries/browse.sql`
  (`ListSeasonEpisodeDetails`), `internal/core/api/browse.go`, `web/apps/web/src/series.tsx`; for
  whole seasons, `identify/series.go`.

## The owner's questions

1. **Phase A's assumptions** (§A.2): under the title, films and series only, links to the filtered
   list?
2. **A person's own ordering** (episode-orderings.md step 7, D159 §2): in B2, or after the UI
   revamp (B4)? The recommendation is after: its editor (move, renumber, omit) is a screen of its
   own, and the DVD clause needs only phase B. Also episode-orderings.md's three flagged points:
   the selector's listed count, `missingFetchesPerSweep` as a constant, one request per series where
   a provider's record cannot carry the list. The recommendation is to keep all three as built.
3. **A toggle in the poster's tray** (closed-beta.md's note) or D370 §4's marking on the pages? The
   recommendation is D370 §4 as built: nothing more in B2.
4. **The watchlist's surfaces.** The recommendation, within D150 §3: *Add to watchlist* / *Remove*
   on a film's and a series' page; a Home row after Next Up; *On my watchlist* in the list pop-up;
   a title leaves the list once watched, and a series once every episode is. No screen of its own,
   no tray cue.
5. **Missing episodes' extent.** The recommendation: listed in seasons the library holds; a whole
   missing season named on the series page from the series record with no new request, its
   episodes listed only once fetched; specials (season 0) only where the library has one; an
   episode not yet aired not listed; no setting to hide them until someone asks (as D370 §6).
6. **Which filters and sorts.** The recommendation: filters by audio language, subtitle language and
   resolution (4K, HD, SD), and *In progress* beside *Unwatched*; sorts by runtime and by date
   watched. Ratings wait for cast, crew and ratings.
7. **Disc folders in B2?** D306 §5 kept them out "unless people start asking". The recommendation:
   VIDEO_TS in B2, as closed-beta.md lists it, since the image's reader does most of it; BDMV and
   `cd1`/`cd2` films after.
8. **The second factor.** The recommendations: TOTP (RFC 6238) first, written on the standard
   library, so no dependency, with ten one-time recovery codes; optional for each person, and an
   admin may require it of admins; asked at the password sign-in and at a realm sign-in through
   Connect, not at a device code's approval, which is made on a device already signed in; a reset
   link never clears it, and `reset-admin`, which needs the machine, does. Passkeys after:
   orpheum-connect has a WebAuthn verifier of its own (`internal/webauthn`), but it is not under the
   core's licence, so using it is the owner's call (rule 3's spirit), as is a dependency instead.
9. **Profiles.** The recommendations: an admin chooses which people appear in a household's picker,
   and an admin account never does (D150 §4); each person may set a 4–6 digit PIN on their own
   profile, and switching to a profile with one asks for it, with the sign-in's failure limit; a TV
   opens on the picker at start, and it is in the account menu elsewhere; a switch issues a session
   for the chosen person on the same device, and signing out ends both; a profile has no password
   unless an admin gives it one. Kids' restrictions (D84) are not in B2.

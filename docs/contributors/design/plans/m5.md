## What M5 asks

cut-a's M5 row (D245):

- **Deliverable:** one skinned web UI, from research into best practice, competitors and what people
  say of them, and into what the web's practice forgot of older UI toolkits; a UI principles
  document, with the standards that touch the UI revisited against it; an architecture supple enough
  to iterate on.
- **Acceptance:** the two people use it in their web browsers, the remote one's voice on her phone's
  browser, and the owner calls it polished. A change to one screen's look needs no change elsewhere.

## Handed on from M4

- **D268 §4: a host's moves are taken at the controls.** The skinned player's play, pause and seek,
  and the Media Session's handlers (lock screen, media keys, the iPhone's own fullscreen), send the
  command, and the follower moves the player as its own move. Element events stay a fallback, read
  by comparing states, not by one-shot marks. This is also the seam for D268 §5: guests given play,
  pause and perhaps seek later.
- **On the Watch Together play page, for everyone:** a volume control for each other participant
  and one for the film, the device's own buttons staying the master volume (the owner, 2026-10-07;
  watch-together.md, "Open").
- **Viewer subtitles in Watch Together,** now under watch-together.md's "Later".

## Questions for the owner, before the first line

From [ui-architecture-2026-10-05.md](../research/ui-architecture-2026-10-05.md) §6:

1. **Is D138 §4's user-facing theming seam still wanted,** now that D245 asks for one skin? If yes,
   class names and tokens are a public surface and stay stable. If no, scoped class names become an
   option and the tokens can change freely.
2. **stylelint, or a Python check,** to enforce the CSS rules? stylelint is a new dependency
   (rule 3); the check is a change to `scripts/` (rule 2).
3. **Which browsers are the floor?** It decides container queries, popover and `@scope`, and the
   Media Session that D268 §4 relies on, and depends on what the Cast receiver and the laptops run.
4. **A behaviour library:** none for now, and which one when the first hard widget comes?
5. **Keyboard arrow navigation** in grids and rows: in Cut A, or with the television milestone?
6. **A specimen screen in the app, or Storybook or Ladle?**

From [ui-design-practice-2026-10-05.md](../research/ui-design-practice-2026-10-05.md) §5.3 (D251
answered its questions 1–3 and 7):

7. **A static resume banner on Home,** or only rows? D188 declined autoplay, not a banner.
8. **A choice of poster size in Cut A,** or one well-chosen size first?
9. **Does the artwork's colour tint the title page,** as Plex and Apple do, or is the page always the
   neutral background?

10. **How "a change to one screen's look needs no change elsewhere" is tested.** cut-a names no test.

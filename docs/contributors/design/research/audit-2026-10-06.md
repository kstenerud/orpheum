# M4's audit: two people, one remote (2026-10-06)

AGENTS.md rule 17 asks for the whole [checklist](../../audit-checklist.md) at every milestone.
This one covers everything since [the audit before M4](audit-2026-10-03.md) (an earlier commit..HEAD`,
about 220 commits): M4's Phases A–H, and what grew beside them (D247–D252). Two read-only reviews
ran in parallel, one on the documents, acceptance and process, one on the code; the coordinator
ran the acceptance itself and checked the findings that matter most at their use sites.

## 0. The verdict

**M4's features are built and work between real people; M4 is not done.** Everything cut-a's M4
row names exists and has tests, and the owner has used it across phones on staging. The netem
acceptance, run for the first time tonight, found one defect in the follower, fixed (§1), and one
contradiction between two decisions that only the owner can settle (§8, Q1). Until Q1 is decided,
the 30-minute acceptance cannot pass under the settings as they stand. Signed-in guests' history
waits on D246 §4 by decision. The documents lag the code by a whole rework of Watch Together
(D246, D250): §4 lists them, and the plain fixes are made as part of this audit.

## 1. Fixed during the audit

- **The follower ignored a message's transit**. A group message was taken as current
  when it arrived, so every follower sat behind the group by its message's way from the server:
  530 ms on the congested profile, while it believed itself in step. D66 has the round trip bound
  a message's age; half the shortest round trip measured is now added. Found by the acceptance.
- **Firefox's voice hung at "Asking for your microphone"** (22fba2c0): opening the microphone
  waited for the audio context to resume, which Firefox keeps pending until it lets the page play
  sound. Found by the browser tier's new voice test.
- **A no-op stylesheet rule** for the logo's link (revert-red; dropped, with a browser test of the
  link instead).

## 5. Names and drift from behaviour (items 7, 8)

- **`adminNote`** on a Watch Together link is the *host's* note, and a host need not be an admin;
  `admin_note` already means the admin's note on an account. It is on the wire: Q4.
- **One value, two names:** the setting `together_drift_left_alone_ms` is `leaveDriftUpToMs` on
  the wire and in Go, so a grep for one misses the other.
- **`turn.ICE`** is one ICE server; `ICEServer`, as the browser calls it, reads right.
- **`cloudflare.ErrTokenRefused`** is also returned when no token is set.
- **`readInto`**'s error says "three missed pings" whatever the silence it was given.
- **`credentialLifetime`** says a membership that ends ends its relay credentials; nothing closes a
  live allocation when a member leaves (checked at the next authenticated TURN request only).

## 6. Derived data, one way, outbound calls, values (items 9, 10, 11, 21, 22)

- **A kept certificate is never checked against `https.domain`**: after the name changes, the old
  name's certificate is served until 30 days before it expires. A defect: §9 lists it to fix.
- **Watch Together's tuning is read once per device**, in its welcome; a changed setting reaches a
  connected device only when it reconnects. Intended for testing (D250 §1), worth saying in the
  setting's description.
- **The voice client keeps ICE credentials past their 24 hours** when a fetch fails.
- **Starting values written three times** (the registry, `startingTuning`, `startingSyncWait`)
  with one equality test among them.
- **"The buffered range around the position"** is written five times with two tolerances
  (`together-follow.ts`, `player/src/link.ts`, `player/src/chunked.ts`); `haveFutureData = 3`
  twice.
- **Cloudflare's zone lookup reads one page of 50**; a token reaching more zones gets `ErrNoZone`,
  which the renewal marks permanent. A defect: §9.
- **ACME has no rate-limit policy** beyond the job's back-off; **reachability** counts a 5xx from
  the VPS's proxy as "another server", and has no overall bound.
- **The ACME directory is production Let's Encrypt, fixed**: staging spends production quota. A
  config key is the obvious home: Q5.
- **Client constants the owner tuned** (`scrubPauseWindowMs`, `refillAheadMs`,
  `partnerGainMost`, `deadAfterUnansweredPings`) are compiled in while their siblings are settings
  in the welcome's tuning (item 22).

## 8. For the owner

- **Q1, the drift left alone.** D250 §1 set `together_drift_left_alone_ms` to 500 ms ("I'm fine
  with leaving up to 500ms"); D243 §9 bounds two clients to 200 ms apart 95% of the time. Both
  cannot hold: a follower 400 ms off is left there. Measured tonight, three minutes a profile,
  with the transit fix: at 500 ms, home sat 430 ms off for good; at 150 ms, home and mobile were
  within 200 ms 100% of the time (95th percentile under 70 ms) and congested 94.4%. Concept
  first: is the bound the promise, with the allowance tuned to it, or is the allowance the
  owner's comfort, with the bound loosened? If the bound: 150 ms, or 100 ms for congested's
  sake, then one 30-minute run to confirm.
- **Q7, voice under the congested profile.** The 30-minute run put the host's voice 1.1 s late (a
  0.9 s jitter buffer) with 4.3% of its samples concealed, against 300 ms and 2%. Part of it is the
  run's own shaping: one 5 Mbit link and its queue carry both browsers' films and both voices, which
  no household shares, and VQ24's per-client shaping on nuc13 will say how much. But 3% random
  loss against a 2% concealment bound is tight on any link. Is congested's voice bound the promise
  (then: Opus's in-band error correction, a smaller film for a guest on a lossy link, or both), or
  is voice on a congested link best-effort?
- **Q2, the short netem run in every branch gate** (D252 §2) needs `NET_ADMIN` in CI's
  container (`.github`). Add it, or leave netem to the milestone run?
- **Q3, signed-in guests' history** (D246 §4): M4's acceptance names it. Build it now, or accept
  M4 without it and carry it?
- **Q4, rename `adminNote` to `hostNote`** on the wire.
- **Q5, the ACME directory as a config key**, so staging uses Let's Encrypt's staging CA.
- **Q6, watch-together.md**: mark it superseded by D246 and D250 at its head, or rewrite its
  Sync and Abilities sections?

# M4's closing audit (2026-10-08)

AGENTS.md rule 17 asks for the whole [checklist](../../audit-checklist.md) at every milestone. This
audit covers an earlier commit..HEAD`, 130 commits from 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08: D253–D269, the
sync review's changes, the chunked player's own bitrate controller, memberships, voice levelling,
and the test and gate work. Two read-only reviews ran in parallel, one on the documents,
acceptance and process, one on the code. The coordinator checked the main findings at their
source before acting on them.

## 0. The verdict

**M4's features are built and work between real people, and M4 is not yet done.** Two things
stand between it and done:

- **A recorded full acceptance run after D268.** The four-profile runs of 2026-10-07 are recorded
  only in the sandbox's notes, and D268 then changed the follower and the group's start.
- **The documents.** They lag the code by D253–D269 (§5). Rule 1 needs the frozen plan to say what
  is true before M5 is built on it.

Thirteen defects were fixed during the audit (§1), two of them live on staging and user-visible.

### Derived values (item 9)

- **A failed full read of a film whose keyframes came from its index is forgotten.**
  `UpsertVideoTimeline` drops the failure once keyframes are kept, so the sweep reads the film
  again every hour. Keeping it needs a column of its own (§10, for the owner). The `failure`
  column would make the film uncuttable.
- **`#loadRatio` is not refreshed at a quality switch,** so catch-up overshoots after a step down.
- **Each `GET /together/mine` mints a credential,** and 16 are kept, so the 17th call evicts a
  device in use.
- **Area stamps see only tracked files.** An ignored input, such as `node_modules` or the Node
  version, does not invalidate one.

## 9. The process (items 12–15)

- **The commit gate never ran in this clone.** `make setup` had not been run, so `core.hooksPath`
  was unset. Three commits went in with the gate failing or the build broken (A26, P56). The shell
  hook now refuses a commit without the hooks.
- **Trailers keep landing on later commits:** `Test-Renamed`, `Test-Weakened` and `Gate-Changed`
  for changes in earlier ones. The commit-msg hook could run the weakening check at the commit
  that makes the change. That is a gate change for the owner.
- **One browser-tier run failed once:** the warm-up MP4 played its original file, not its ladder,
  under the check-pr load. It passed twice alone and in the full run after. It is a timing test
  under load near `play_ladder_wait_ms`; no cause was found.
- **New gates** (documentation-only, area stamps, `vitest related`, index-only reads): whether each
  was seen failing on every kind of file it claims was not checked. A test of each failing case
  exists.
- **Agent failures:** A25 (a design choice called a necessity) and A26 (commits without the gate)
  were added, each with its practice or gate.
- **Plan deviations:** P51–P56 were added.
- **The verification queue:**
  - unticked: VQ1, VQ7, VQ9–VQ11, VQ13–VQ19 and VQ21–VQ27;
  - could become tests: VQ25's webkit events, synthetic, and part of VQ24, by network namespaces
    in the development container.

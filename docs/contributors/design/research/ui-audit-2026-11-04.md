# UI audit: the report

- **Date:** 2026-11-04. **Asked by** the owner: "consulting experts, studies, and competitors to
  see what kind of information should be accessible in what number of clicks, where focus should
  land on pages, how the controls should be layed out for best UX".
- **Built from:** [ui-audit-2026-11-04-inventory.md](ui-audit-2026-11-04-inventory.md) (every page
  on staging, desktop, phone, Pixel and Fire TV; screenshots in `/yoloai/files/ui-audit-2026-11-04/`)
  and [ui-audit-2026-11-04-research.md](ui-audit-2026-11-04-research.md) (studies, platform
  guidance and competitors, with sources).
- **Status:** a proposal. Nothing here is decided until the owner answers §4; the answers become
  a decision and the revamp's plan.

## 1. What is wrong, in five lines

1. **The main action is rarely first.** A film's Play is the last control on its page, below the
   fold at 1366×768, on phones and on the TV. The desktop player's play/pause row is below the fold
   even at 1920×1080.
2. **Nothing is focused when a page opens**, on any device. On the TV every page costs a blind
   first press, and the press often lands somewhere unhelpful (Films: Search, then the grid's last
   column).
3. **The arrows skip controls.** The focus engine considers only controls in line and has no
   fallback (`web/apps/web/src/focus-engine.ts`), so Down passes the series' Play, the controller
   choices, Account's Activate and the episode's links; Right at a row's end jumps into the bar.
4. **Growing lists bury what follows them.** Account (7,164 px; 19,006 on a phone), a person
   (6,621), Jobs (30,393) and System (7,497) have no paging, folding or contents.
5. **Similar pages are laid out differently and named differently**: film, episode and series pages
   place Play three ways; one library is "Films", "All Films" and "Movies".

## 2. Rules the revamp follows (proposed)

From the research's §6.1, kept where the inventory shows a page breaking them:

| # | Rule | Broken today by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Budgets: play from a title page in 1 press, resume from Home in 1–2, any page crossed in at most 6 presses | film page (4 to Play), Account (230 stops to its settings) |
| R2 | Broad, labelled levels: about 8 items, 10–15 settings a screen | Settings, System, Account |
| R3 | Arrival focuses the main action on TV and keyboard; no ring for mouse and touch | every page |
| R4 | A screen reader hears the new page's heading on arrival | every page |
| R5 | Back restores the last focus in each area; a closed dialog returns focus to its opener | series → episode → Back; the paused player's first Back |
| R6 | The arrow search falls back to the nearest control off the line | the focus engine |
| R7 | Straight-path layouts: one axis per job, aligned rows, one actions row, no orphan controls | Mark Season n as watched, See all, the code field |
| R8 | A detail page reads title, facts, actions row (Play first), description, then the rest; no tabs | film page |
| R9 | A settings page is one column, either instant or one Save at the end | Settings (raw keys, fields 1,500 px away), a library's Scans |
| R10 | Phone: main places in a visible bar; the main action in thumb reach | the icon-only bar; Play 1.4 screens down |

R3, R5 and R6 are checked by a test that walks every page's controls by arrows and fails on one it
cannot reach (Android TV's checklist; principles §17).

## 3. Each page

Depth is clicks on desktop / presses on the TV from Home. "Now" is from the inventory.

### The shell

- **Now:** icons without labels; the bar plus Silk's toolbar take 240 of 1,080 pixels on the TV;
  the tester's menu opens on Device tests; the admin's group has no heading.
- **Proposed:** labelled places (Home, Films, Series, Search, and the menu), as a top bar or side
  rail (question Q1). The menu lists the viewer's items first (This device, Your account, About,
  Switch household, Sign out), then a **Manage** heading for the admin, and moves Device tests
  under About or This device. Search becomes a place of its own instead of an icon on each list.
  Watch Together and Cast get distinct icons and labels.

### Sign in

- **Now:** nothing focused; Realm first though most people type it once.
- **Proposed:** focus the first empty field (Name when the realm is remembered). Keep the order.

### Home

- **Now:** focus on body; a Hide stop under every Continue watching card; "See all" 1,800 px from
  its heading; New films below the TV's fold.
- **Proposed:** arrival focuses the first Continue watching card (or the first new title). Hide
  moves into the card's menu (a long press, or More), so Down leaves a row in one press. "See all"
  sits beside the row's heading. Rows: Continue watching, then the libraries' new titles.

### Films, Series and Libraries (`/films`, `/series`, `/browse`)

- **Now:** the first Down lands on Search, the next on the grid's last column; Right from the last
  column enters the A–Z rail; two routes with three names to the same list.
- **Proposed:** arrival focuses the first poster. Search and Sort sit in a row above the grid at its
  left, reached by Up. The A–Z rail is reached by Right from the last column only on purpose (a
  press past the end), or replaced by Sort plus jump-to-letter. One name per library everywhere;
  Films and Series stay as the shell's places, `/browse` becomes "Libraries" for the folders an
  admin made (question Q7).

### A film's page

- **Now:** breadcrumb, a 400 px backdrop, poster, title, facts, tagline, synopsis, then Versions
  holding Play and File details, then Mark as watched; no cast, director, genres, rating, resume
  point, audio or subtitle choice. Play is the 4th press on TV, below the fold at 1366 and on
  phones.
- **Proposed (R8):** the backdrop behind the first screen, not above it. Title; one facts line
  (year, length, rating, quality, sound); **the actions row: Play or Resume (with the point) first,
  Start over when resuming, Mark as watched, More** (File details, versions, admin actions);
  synopsis clamped with More; below: cast and crew, versions and technical detail. Arrival focuses
  Play. Several versions: Play plays the best one; the choice lives under More.

### An episode's page

- **Now:** no backdrop, Play near the top; the series and season links under the title skipped by
  Down.
- **Proposed:** the same template as a film's page, with the series and season as a breadcrumb line
  above the title, and Next episode in the actions row. Arrival focuses Play.

### A series and its seasons

- **Now:** Play S2E1 beside the synopsis (good), but Down from the top passes it; Mark Season n as
  watched at the far right, 1,700 px from the episodes; Up from the first episode lands sideways.
- **Proposed:** header and an actions row (Resume or Play next, Mark as watched, More); seasons as
  one row of tabs-as-buttons; the season's episodes as a column below, each row with its progress;
  Mark season as watched goes into the season's own row or More. Arrival focuses Resume / Play next;
  Back from an episode returns to it. Changing season is not a history step.

### The player

- **Now:** on desktop the player sits inside a scrolling page, play/pause below the fold, with
  loudness, sync, dialogue boost, tracks and picture timing under it. On the TV focus sits on the
  hidden timeline at load, and the first Back after pausing shows the page under the player.
- **Proposed:** full window on every device, controls over the picture. Bottom row: timeline; play/
  pause, back and forward 10 s; Audio and subtitles (one panel, 3–4 presses to change a track);
  Settings (loudness, dialogue boost, sound sync, quality); Full screen. Diagnostics (tracks,
  picture timing) move behind Settings → For testers, or to Device tests. Arrival: no ring, controls
  hidden; the first press shows them with play/pause focused. Back hides the controls if shown, else
  leaves: never a third state.

### Watch Together and Cast dialogs

- **Now:** focus on Close in Cast; Tab stops on the page body inside both.
- **Proposed:** focus the first device / Start a session; remove the body stop. Not walked live: a
  session and a cast remote need the second device and are to be walked during the revamp.

### Your account

- **Now:** the settings sit under an unbounded device list; Down skips the name field and Activate.
- **Proposed:** order by frequency: Activate a device (code, name, Activate in one row); Playing;
  Change password; Your server at home; then **Your devices**, showing the five most recent with
  "All devices (115)" opening the full list; Sign out everywhere else with it; Testing last or moved
  to This device. Arrival focuses the code field.

### This device

- **Now:** fits the screen; Down skips the controller choices.
- **Proposed:** keep; fixed by R6. Arrival focuses Rename.

### About

- **Proposed:** its heading becomes an h1; no other change.

### Manage (the admin's pages)

- **Needs a match, Libraries, People, What the scanner saw:** fit and work. Arrival focuses the
  first item; nothing else.
- **A library:** the irreversible "This folder is gone for good…" is the first control. Move each
  folder's two repair actions under a More per folder, with the least harmful choice focused in its
  dialog; put Scan now first.
- **Create a library:** one long form, Create and scan below the fold at 1366. Group it (name and
  kind; source; scanning) with Create and scan at the end, reached by Down from the last field.
- **A person:** the devices bury Remove account. The same treatment as Your account: five devices
  and "All devices", the account's actions in a header row.
- **Settings:** raw keys as labels, fields 1,500 px away. One column: a human label above each
  field, the key in small type under it, the field under the label; groups as now. Save per group or
  instant per field (question Q5).
- **Jobs:** 30,393 px with no filter. Running and queued first, then finished folded by day, with a
  filter by kind and state.
- **System:** 7,497 px with no contents. A contents row at the top linking each section, the
  actions (Back up now, Check now) in each section's heading row, and the long tables folded.
- **How to update:** no menu leads to it. Link it from Settings' heading sentence and from Needs
  attention.

## 4. Questions for the owner

From the research (§6.2) and the inventory:

1. **TV menu: top bar or left side rail?** The evidence is split (Netflix moved to the top in 2025,
   Max to a rail). A top bar holds five or six labelled places at 960 px; more needs a rail.
   *Recommendation:* top bar with Home, Films, Series, Search, Watch Together, Menu: six, and it
   matches desktop.
2. **Phones: bottom bar or top bar?** Bottom is in thumb reach but sits against the browser's own
   toolbar. *Recommendation:* bottom, with the safe-area inset.
3. **Right at the end of a grid's short last row: stay, wrap, or go up?** You chose stay on
   2026-10-25. *Recommendation:* keep stay.
4. **Should a focused poster grow to show its facts (Netflix 2025)?** *Recommendation:* not in the
   revamp; the title page carries them, and it adds motion on slow TVs.
5. **Which settings apply at once and which have Save?** *Recommendation:* personal ones (This
   device, Playing, the player's settings) at once; admin forms (a library, a person, Settings) one
   Save per group.
6. **With a mouse or touch, should a page focus anything on arrival?** *Recommendation:* yes, the
   same target as the TV, with no ring shown (`:focus-visible`), so keyboard users start there.
7. **One name per library:** should the shell's Films and Series stay as places beside a Libraries
   list, or should the libraries themselves (Movies, Musicals, Shows) be the places?
   *Recommendation:* Films and Series as the places, filtered by library, since most households
   have one of each.
8. **Diagnostics on the player page and Device tests in the menu:** move both behind a "For
   testers" entry? *Recommendation:* yes; the closed beta's testers keep one press more.

AirPlay lab

Four short runs, about 20 minutes in all. Use the iPhone as the sender and the Mac as the receiver. Turn the Mac's sound up. Every page sends its log to the Mac by itself, so there's nothing to copy: just tell me what you heard and saw (the yellow boxes) after each run.

The test picture is a counter, and the sound is a beep each second. The low beep is the first sound, and the high beep is the second.

Run 1: WebKit's own handover (decides design B)

Open run 1's page
  1. Press Load hls.js, then B: hls.js, alternative at attach, then Play.
  2. After about 10 s, press AirPlay (webkit picker) and choose the Mac.
  3. Watch for 20 s.
  4. Press AirPlay (webkit picker) again and choose the iPhone. Watch for 20 s.
  5. Reload the page. Press Load hls.js, then C: hls.js, no alternative (now), then Play.
  6. Press C: add the alternative now, then AirPlay (webkit picker), and choose the Mac. Watch for 20 s, then choose the iPhone again.

Tell me: In step 2, did the Mac play, and from where the counter was on the iPhone? In step 4, did the iPhone carry on playing by itself? In step 6, did the Mac play?

Run 1b: the same, with hls.js 1.7.4

This needs hls-1.7.4.js in this folder. (Under 1.7.3, WebKit switched to the alternative, then hls.js reset it and ended AirPlay.)

Open run 1b's page

Do run 1's steps 1–6 again. After each Load hls.js, check that the log says loaded 1.7.4.

Tell me the same three things as for run 1.

Run 2: changing the sound during AirPlay (fault 1)

Open run 2's page
  1. Press Load full master, then Play. Listen: is it the low beep or the high beep?
  2. Press AirPlay (webkit picker) and choose the Mac. Which beep does the Mac play?
  3. Press Enable sound 0 (Low tone) and listen to the Mac for 10 s. Then press Enable sound 1 (High tone) and listen for 10 s.
  4. Press Reload here: low-only and listen for 15 s. Then press Reload here: high-only and listen for 15 s.
  5. Choose the iPhone in the picker to end.

Tell me: Which beep played in steps 1 and 2? Did either press in step 3 change the Mac's beep? In step 4, did the Mac change beep, stay on AirPlay, and keep its counter, and about how long was it silent?

Run 3: the picker and Stop (fault 2)

Open run 3's page
  1. Press Load and play. Press AirPlay (webkit picker) and close the picker without choosing anything. Wait 10 s.
  2. Press AirPlay (webkit picker) and choose the Mac. Wait 10 s.
  3. Press AirPlay (webkit picker) and close it without choosing anything. Wait 10 s.
  4. Press disableRemotePlayback = true. Wait 10 s.
  5. Press AirPlay (webkit picker) and choose the iPhone.

Tell me: In step 3, did the Mac carry on playing? In step 4, did the Mac stop showing the picture, and where was the sound?

Run 4 (if you have time): pauses nobody pressed

Open run 4's page
  1. Load it and press Play, turn on Log the position every second, then AirPlay to the Mac.
  2. Press the page's Pause, then Play, then Seek +30 s. Wait 5 s after each.
  3. Pause and play once from the Mac's own controls.
  4. Lock the iPhone for 30 s, then unlock it.

Tell me: Did anything pause that you didn't press? And roughly how long did the Mac take to follow each press?

When you're done

Stop the server on the Mac (Ctrl-C). In the airplay-lab folder, run zip -r logs.zip logs, copy logs.zip to the machine this sandbox runs on, and there run yoloai files orpheum put logs.zip. Then send me your answers from the yellow boxes.

# Name check: "Orpheum"

Checked 2026-09-23. **This is not legal advice.** It is a quick screen by a non-lawyer, not a clearance opinion. Anything marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be confirmed from a primary source. Raw responses are cached in `/yoloai/cache/http/name-orpheum/`.

## Verdict

| Use | Risk |
|---|---|
| (a) Private use now (private repo, own devices) | **Low** |
| (b) Public open-source release (GitHub, Docker, sideloaded APK) | **Low–medium** |
| (c) Google Play / Amazon / App Store listings and a paid hosted relay | **Medium** |

No live registration found covers **ORPHEUM for software, streaming, or SaaS** (Nice classes 9-software, 38, 42) in the US (USPTO) or in TMview's worldwide index. The name is still crowded, for three reasons:
1. **Bose Professional** has new ORPHEUM filings in class 9 (audio hardware) in the US, Canada and under the Madrid system.
2. A **German ORPHEUM** word mark (2025) covers class 9 audio and visual apparatus.
3. Many **Orpheum theatres** exist, one of which holds US class 41 registrations.

None of these blocks the project today. Two things would be hard: registering the project's own ORPHEUM trademark in class 9 or 41, and ranking in search. The domains are mostly gone: .com, .org, .net, .app and .io are all taken.

### Confusion risk for software and streaming

- **Bose Pro ORPHEUM, class 9, US and international.** The goods differ: commercial loudspeakers and amplifiers against media-server software. Both are still "audio" in class 9, and Bose Pro sells control software, so the channels touch. The main realistic problem is an **examiner citation or an opposition if the project files ORPHEUM in class 9**, not a lawsuit over simply using the name. Risk: **low–medium.** With a music player added later, it rises slightly.
- **German Weinert mark, class 9 "audio devices and visual apparatus".** This is broad hardware wording. An EU or German class 9 filing by the project could draw an opposition. Risk for use: low. Risk for EU registration: **medium.**
- **Los Angeles Orpheum Theatre, class 41 live entertainment.** A media server is not a live venue. However, a hosted relay filed in class 38 or 41 ("streaming of video/music") is closer to "entertainment", and theatre owners are touchy about their names. Risk: **low**, rising to low–medium for a paid hosted "entertainment" service.
- **ORPHEUM WIEN (classes 9/41/42).** This is the closest on paper because class 42 is included, but its goods are **UNVERIFIED**. Check it before any EU filing.

## 4. Linguistic and search issues

- **Meaning.** "Orpheum" is a Latinised form built on Orpheus, the mythic musician, in the pattern of odeum/odeon, the classical hall for music. Around 1900 it became a stock name for vaudeville and cinema theatres through the Orpheum Circuit, which merged into RKO in 1928. For a place where you watch films and listen to music, the meaning fits well. It suits video now and music later better than "Odeon" or anything cinema-only would.
- **Pronunciation and spelling.** It is said "OR-fee-um". People will type or hear "Orpheus", "Orphium" or "Orfeum". Misspellings lead to the Orpheus results above, including the piracy tracker and the ripping tool.
- **Searchability: poor.** "orpheum" alone will be dominated by theatre box offices: Los Angeles, San Francisco (BroadwaySF), Minneapolis (Hennepin Arts), Memphis, Vancouver, Omaha, Boston, Sydney's Hayden Orpheum, Vienna and others. Their own apps even show up for "orpheum" in the App Store. Compound queries like "orpheum media server", "orpheum jellyfin" or "orpheum app" currently have almost no competition, apart from Neotica's small music app. The project would need a consistent descriptor to be findable. This is an estimate from the app store and GitHub results; web search was unavailable in this session, so **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Other languages.** No negative meaning found. It reads as "Orpheum" or "Orfeum" across European languages, and "Orfeo" is Italian and Spanish for Orpheus. The myth ends with Orpheus losing Eurydice by looking back and being torn apart, but few people will make that connection. No problem found in Chinese, Japanese or Korean: the existing CJK marks are coffee and cosmetics brands. The check was limited, so **UNVERIFIED** beyond these points.

## 5. Recommendation and mitigations

- **(a) Private use now: low risk.** Keep going.
- **(b) Public open-source release: low–medium.** The risks come from search ranking and the Orpheus misspellings, not from law. Always present the name with a descriptor: **"Orpheum Media Server"**, with clients named "Orpheum for Android TV" and so on. Never call the product just "Orpheum Theatre" or anything similar, and use no theatre-style imagery such as marquees or vaudeville art. That imagery would invite a claim of association from the Los Angeles registrant or other venues.
- **(c) App stores and a paid hosted relay: medium.** The Bose Pro and German class 9 marks, the class 41 theatre marks and the Vienna class 9/41/42 mark make **registering** ORPHEUM on its own uncertain. They make a quiet **coexistence** likely. Store reviewers act on complaints, not on searches.

**Do now (cheap):**
1. Register **orpheum.dev** and **orpheum.tv**, plus one of getorpheum.com or orpheumserver.com. Price-check **orpheum.io** and **orpheum.net** on Afternic.
2. Create a GitHub org such as `orpheum-media` or `orpheumserver`, since `orpheum` is taken. Claim the Docker Hub namespace (try `orpheum`, otherwise `orpheummedia`) and the npm name `orpheum`.
3. Pick the reverse-DNS ID for Play and Apple bundles now (for example `dev.orpheum.*`, if you own orpheum.dev), because it can never change after the first Play upload.

**Before the Play Store launch or the paid relay:**
4. Commission a **professional clearance search** covering US, EU, UK and Canada, classes 9, 38, 41 and 42. Ask specifically about Bose Pro IR 1848520 (its designated countries), DE 3020252211086, AT 1717/2001 and Neotica's app.
5. Consider filing a composite mark, **ORPHEUM MEDIA SERVER** or a logo mark, in classes 9 and 42, with any 41 filing narrowed to "providing non-downloadable video via a hosted relay". Keep the goods well away from loudspeakers, amplifiers and live performances.
6. Put the relay under a separate sub-brand, for example "Orpheum Relay", so a later rename of the paid part does not force a rename of the open-source project.

**Rename trigger.** Rename if the clearance search finds that the Bose Pro international registration covers the EU and UK with software wording, or if Neotica's Orpheum music app files a mark in class 9 or 42. Either would move (c) to high, so decide before the first store listing, while a rename is still cheap. Alternative names were not screened, because the overall risk did not come out high.
